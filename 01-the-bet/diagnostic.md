# Three-Axis Vulnerability Diagnostic

## Product

**Product:** BJJ Ready to Go — an AI competition preparation copilot for Brazilian jiu-jitsu athletes, combining Garmin data, weight trends, training logs, and competition requirements.
**Your Role:** Product owner (course project).

---

## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 2/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:** Competition preparation could become a daily habit, but strong workflow integration and switching costs have not yet been established. Athletes could currently switch easily.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):** Garmin Connect — initial candidate; to be challenged with a partner.

---

### Data Advantage — 1/5
*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:** No proprietary dataset has been established linking preparation, athlete decisions, and competition outcomes. Imported Garmin data alone does not create a unique advantage. Longitudinal BJJ-specific feedback could become an advantage, but this is unvalidated.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):** Garmin — initial candidate, given its source wearable ecosystem; to be challenged with a partner.

---

### Platform Exposure — 2/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:** Low resilience to platform encroachment: Garmin could add similar competition guidance within its ecosystem. This is a hypothetical threat, not a claim about an announced feature. Deeper BJJ context and coach workflows could differentiate us, but neither is proven.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):** Garmin Connect — initial candidate; to be challenged with a partner.

---

## Top Vulnerability
We rely on Garmin data without a proven proprietary data advantage or strong switching costs.

## Confidence Level
**L — Low.** Initial assessment of the product concept; scores and competitive assumptions have not yet been validated with athletes or a partner challenge.
